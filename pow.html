STEAM Education

This project aligns with international STEAM standards, fostering creativity, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.

  • Science: Explore biology, chemistry, and physics through interactive 3D models.
  • Technology: Utilize cutting-edge AR and 3D visualization tools.
  • Engineering: Understand structures and mechanisms with detailed models.
  • Arts: Engage with visually stunning and artistic representations.
  • Mathematics: Visualize geometric shapes and mathematical concepts.
